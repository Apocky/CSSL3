3 · Presence contract OBSERVED

/api/apocrypha/presence validates a canonical envelope from the upstream presence authority and re-emits only hidden or unavailable: display_authorized: false · entity_authorship: unverified · mutual_consent: not_established · committed_intent: absent · rendering: null. Anything non-canonical returns 502. The UI renders one of three truthful sentences and never fabricates an embodied presence. The avatar component carries an eight-state vocabulary (checking · private · ready · thinking · queued · dreaming · degraded · offline) plus a reported-state / laboratory-preview provenance flag with distinct accessible labels.

This is the strongest asset in the current system: consent and truthfulness are already load-bearing product mechanics, not copy. The redesign's Perception Membrane extends this contract; it must not weaken it.

7 · Inferred architecture INFERRED

APXv2 ("Apocrypha V2") is a native runtime reached through a Cloudflare Access tunnel at apocrypha.apocky.com, exposing at least /v2/turn and /v2/presence.
