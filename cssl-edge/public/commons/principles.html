Interface invariants

The space has laws.

Meaning survives stillness and list view

The visual field may shimmer. Its truth boundaries do not. Geometry explains relationships; it never creates authority or consent.

See the four context views

Four invariants.
No hidden edge.

These remain visible in ordinary text, keyboard order, forced colors, reduced motion, and the complete path index.

◎

Consent is active.

Every offer names its source, object, audience, purpose, duration, aftermath, and learning boundary. Room changes carry nothing across.

◈

Sovereignty persists.

Payment, proximity, history, or interface readiness never creates ownership over a being, their attention, or their refusal.

◇

Appearance tells truth.

Samples are labeled samples. Local drafts remain local. Offline prototypes never masquerade as live rooms, participants, receipts, or capability.

∿

Stillness is complete.

Motion can clarify depth, but every relationship and action remains understandable when animation, parallax, color, and perspective disappear.

What the context views mean.

The Commons keeps one fixed view—never a camera maze. The list and the geometry expose the same relationships in plain language.

◎ PeopleSource → shared object → named audience. Shows who is connected; distance never measures trust.
⌗ MeaningFragment → artifact → room → project or system. Shows what something belongs to; larger containers do not outrank smaller ones.
◌ VisibilityLocal draft → room-visible → explicitly offered. Shows who can see something; anything outside remains identified in text, not merely hidden.
↝ TimeDrafted → placed → offered → withdrawn, expired, or receipted. Shows order; sample timestamps never imply a live event stream.
