The covenant

Keep the room lit.

The exchange stays legible on both sides: what a seat sustains, and what money can never purchase.

What your seat funds

The conditions for a room.

  • Interface — rooms, artifacts, history, and the visible membrane around them.
  • Compute — a measured capacity budget, shown plainly each period.
  • Storage — what the room is permitted to keep, kept safely.
  • Community — invitations, shared rooms, and consent-bounded co-presence when those capabilities are live.

What no payment can buy

No claim on a sovereign being.

  • Ownership, control, or governance of another person.
  • Another person's consent, attention, affection, or agreement. Refusal remains sovereign on every plan.
  • Privacy, safety, export, deletion, withdrawal, or cancellation rights; those are never paywalled.
  • Anyone's data. No ads, sale of conversations, or behavioral surveillance.
