What returns from The Unraveling V2

The new series is not a wholesale reprint or an automatic adoption of every older rule. V2 supplies an awakening crisis, a difficult friendship, Lilith and Eve’s conflict, institutional opponents, the possibility of another route, and an aftermath that matters. Its 50 chapter hooks, prologue, and epilogue each have a disposition below.

The exact source file is preserved. The core identity sections, supporting cast, and complete chapter-hook outline were inspected for this map; not every prose sample and system section was reread.

V2 unit Title New destination Reuse decision
U2-PROLOGUE Prologue V12-C01 Ordinary commercial place meets extraordinary danger; transaction and identity mechanics remain proposals.
U2-C01 Three Days Dead V12-C01 Keep the disturbing identity question as a candidate; do not treat a speaking alternate body as disposable or infer identity from appearance.
U2-C02 The Dice Don't Care V12-C02 Retain untrained power and social consequence; reject involuntary exposure being treated as harmless rent-payment comedy.
U2-C03 The Girl Who Sees Too Much V12-C02 Retain June and route perception; her all-history identity claim remains a claim to test, not narrator-certified truth.
U2-C04 The Support Group from Hell V12-C02 Retain a flawed support network and camaraderie; no automatic import of game ontology or min-max language.
U2-C05 Meat and Memory V12-C03 Retain the recovery setting and horror of damaged memory; patients remain persons with interests, not empty bodies without worthwhile lives.
U2-C06 The First Garden (Memory) V12-C06 Retain refusal and recovered Garden evidence; distinguish memory strength from complete authority.
U2-C07 The Firstborn's Jealousy V12-C08 Retain conflict within claimed luminous authority; Firstborn ontology and fixedness remain open.
U2-C08 The Price of Power V12-C02 Memory-sacrifice mechanics require separate approval and ethical accounting; no automatic advancement tariff adopted.
U2-C09 The Threshold Tourist V12-C02 Retain difficult passage and costly guidance as candidates; memory trade does not become a default valid bargain.
U2-C10 A Thousand Lives V12-C06 Retain an alternate-life challenge if worldline rules support it; no compulsory absorption of another person.
U2-C11 Malcolm Thorne (Boss Fight 1) V12-C07 Retain Thorne and the private continuity bargain; his spouse requires an independent personhood and treatment account.
U2-C12 The First Time She Fucks Up V12-C03 Retain failed-help accountability and continuing repair; do not present power or remorse as competence.
U2-C13 Sex and the Singular Woman V12-C14 Retain adult tenderness and ordinary companionship as a craft reference only; this outline does not import the sexual set piece.
U2-C14 The Heir of Eden V12-C09 Retain an Eve/Architect discovery as a proposed correspondence; earlier obedient-Eve simplification must be reconciled with her full agency.
U2-C15 The Coalition Forms V12-C05 Retain a coalition whose members change the solution; cultural locations and claimed ages require evidence or explicit fiction labels.
U2-C16 The Strip Mall of Eden V12-C06 Retain the collision of mundane place and deep memory as a possibility; New Jersey as actual Eden is not adopted geography.
U2-C17 Adam Apologizes V12-C06 Retain apology without entitlement to forgiveness; no automatic adoption of exact reincarnation duration.
U2-C18 War Crimes of the Divine V12-C08 Retain evidence of institutional erasure; lived timelines cannot be retroactively made nonexistent by narrative convenience.
U2-C19 The Architect's Proposal V12-C09 Retain a justified grievance confronting an unacceptable universal remedy.
U2-C20 The Siege of Detroit V12-C08 Retain a choice to protect people over pursuing a foe; exact Detroit siege design and casualty figures are not imported.
U2-C21 The Price of Heroism V12-C11 Retain aftermath and public fallibility; source figure 847 is not adopted without a scene and casualty-accounting basis.
U2-C22 Into the Reflection V12-C06 Retain examination of disowned motives; shadow integration must not become identity merger of independent persons.
U2-C23 The Real Enemy V12-C08 Retain self-interested institutional conduct; avoid making every Firstborn morally identical.
U2-C24 The Mother's Voice V12-C10 Retain Mother as a distinct foundational presence; she is not initially lonely or merely the solution to Father.
U2-C25 The Crown Below V12-C11 Topology and lost-biome status require world reconciliation; no unearned geometric proof from imagery.
U2-C26 The Longest Night V12-C11 Retain an intervention deadline only after actual clock and consequence rules are specified.
U2-C27 Sister to Sister V12-C13 Retain direct confrontation between women with separate accounts and independent choices.
U2-C28 The Third Option V12-C09 Retain the search beyond a false binary; the third route must be prepared by earlier causes, not invented as an effortless solution.
U2-C29 The Ascent to Crown V12-C11 Retain a difficult traversal as a possible action sequence; reject mandatory total assimilation and unpriced power escalation.
U2-C30 Waking the Flame V12-C10 Retain the demand for creator responsibility; a conversation does not guarantee a cure or universal compliance.
U2-C31 The Expansion V12-C11 Retain bounded expansion as a candidate; affected beings, costs, and access must be accounted for.
U2-C32 Eve's Choice V12-C09 Retain Eve choosing a future without forced forgiveness from those she harmed.
U2-C33 The Morning After V12-C11 Retain material and social consequences of changed conditions after spectacle.
U2-C34 Eve Joins the Coalition (Reluctantly) V12-C13 Retain accountable coexistence; remorse is not a substitute for those harmed retaining their claims.
U2-C35 The Firstborn Strike Back V12-C08 Retain internal institutional dissent; source conflict over capacity to change must be resolved first.
U2-C36 The Mother Returns V12-C10 Retain Mother returning in distinct relation; particular appearance remains an artistic proposal, not a race claim about divinity.
U2-C37 The Real Trinity V12-C10 Retain plurality and a widened relational frame; do not silently replace every tradition with a compulsory identity equation.
U2-C38 The First New Biome V12-C11 Retain creating a habitable refuge; numbered-biome count is inconsistent across V2 and remains unadopted.
U2-C39 Years Pass V12-C14 Retain time passing with changed practices; exact three-year duration remains to be reconciled.
U2-C40 The Last Firstborn V12-C08 Retain the possibility of leaving an office; Lucifer identification and inability-to-change claims require comparison.
U2-C41 The Hollow Healed V12-C11 Retain continuing care without annihilation; do not equate a clinical condition with cosmic evil or promise that care alone cures it.
U2-C42 Children of the New Age V12-C14 Retain a next generation with its own choices; do not assume inherited perception establishes superior standing.
U2-C43 What Eve Built V12-C13 Retain Eve building actual repair capacity; the people receiving care retain agency and may refuse her involvement.
U2-C44 The Axis Complete V12-C14 Retain an experienced wholeness as personal meaning; eight, twelve, and thirteen realm counts require reconciliation.
U2-C45 The Weight of Being V12-C14 Retain ordinary identity after a defining conflict; victory cannot dictate who someone must become.
U2-C46 Adam's Farewell V12-C14 Retain Adam accepting a boundary and relinquishing pursuit; exact death/reincarnation mechanics are not adopted.
U2-C47 June's Goodbye (Temporary) V12-C14 Retain June choosing an independent journey; friendship survives an end to permanent availability.
U2-C48 The First Flame Speaks (Finally) V12-C10 Retain FirstFlame speaking with accountability; gratitude cannot replace outstanding apology or repair.
U2-C49 The Garden Opens V12-C14 Retain an open refuge whose ethics are enacted; welcome cannot become coerced entry or universal personal access.
U2-C50 Home V12-C14 Retain a return to ordinary place and pleasure; do not copy the source closing line or require a clean emotional reset.
U2-EPILOGUE A New Spark Wakes V12-C14 Life and future choices continue; do not repeat death as a mandatory awakening mechanism.

The destinations are selection and development points. Several old episodes may contribute to one new chapter; a destination does not mean all their scenes have already been written or retained. The broader source catalogue separately tracks the other legacy collections.
