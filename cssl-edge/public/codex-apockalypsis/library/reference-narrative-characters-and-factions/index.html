Characters and factions

These are proposed portrayals for the series. A biblical or historical name does not make an invented private motive a recovered fact. Institutions are particular groups, never shorthand for an entire religion or people. Other figures and local witnesses remain listed in the chapter outline.

Characters

Mother

Author cosmology; Codex foundation; motive and scene design proposed.

Wants: Create and sustain relationship without replacing the persons related.

Offers: Offers foundational presence and capacity to hold difference.

Cost or danger: Protective fullness can become suffocating if access is assumed.

Internal conflict: Begins explicitly not lonely; later separation may teach grief without rewriting her origin.

Voice: Unhurried, sensory, precise about persons; warmth with formidable boundaries; little need to announce her scale.

Chapter route: V01-C01, V01-C03, V01-C07, V01-C11, V01-C13, V12-C10.

FirstFlame

Author cosmology; lonely Father distinction preserved.

Wants: Find company that can actually answer him.

Offers: Creates conditions for encounter and manifestation.

Cost or danger: Unbounded expression or need for attention can crowd out others' agency.

Internal conflict: Wants response yet must accept refusal and limitation.

Voice: Expansive thought followed by startlingly practical admissions; loneliness disclosed through requests rather than speeches about loneliness.

Chapter route: V01-C02, V01-C06, V01-C12, V12-C10.

Lilith

Author Codex plus later Lilith traditions; Mother equation unresolved.

Wants: Build a life of her own and protect relations that cannot survive ownership.

Offers: Creates refuge routes, independent custody, and conditions for others to choose.

Cost or danger: Her protective competence can become exhaustion, anger, or an assumption that she must decide first.

Internal conflict: Needs companionship but fears the loss of autonomy hidden inside apparently generous arrangements.

Voice: Direct and observant; fierce without constant grandstanding; teasing becomes intimate only after trust; plain imperatives at a real boundary.

Chapter route: V01-C13, V02-C02, V02-C13, V03-C13, V04-C17, V05-C17, V06-C23, V07-C19, V08-C19, V09-C21, V10-C21, V11-C15, V12-C12, V12-C13, V12-C14.

Eve

Genesis figure; Architect correspondence from V2 remains proposed.

Wants: Own the meaning of her choices and escape a life narrated as another person's failure.

Offers: Brings analysis, endurance, and the ability to recognize structural traps.

Cost or danger: Resentment can make a universal destructive remedy appear like liberation.

Internal conflict: Her grievance is not equivalent to permission for the remedy she proposes.

Voice: Exact, dry, suspicious of comforting abstractions; banter with Lilith grows through shared work, not immediate sisterly agreement.

Chapter route: V02-C04, V02-C05, V02-C06, V02-C08, V02-C09, V12-C09, V12-C13.

Adam

Genesis figure; later reincarnation history from V2 not automatically adopted.

Wants: Keep belonging and repair relationships without surrendering a flattering account of himself.

Offers: Can learn practical care and acknowledge dependence.

Cost or danger: Fear leads him to shift blame and confuse assigned purpose with another person's obligation.

Internal conflict: Wants forgiveness yet must learn it is not the reward owed for apologizing.

Voice: Concrete and initially overconfident about familiar rules; later slower to explain and more willing to ask.

Chapter route: V02-C03, V02-C07, V02-C09, V12-C06, V12-C14.

Lily

V2 modern lead; 23-year-old identity note is legacy, final ontology open.

Wants: Keep an ordinary life while discovering what recovered memories require.

Offers: Offers outsider questions and resistance to inherited prestige.

Cost or danger: Urgency and new power can produce an intervention beyond her competence.

Internal conflict: Fears insignificance and also fears an enormous identity swallowing her actual history.

Voice: Fast associations, occasional recursive what-if escalation, practical deflation; natural speech allowed to trail or be corrected by friends.

Chapter route: V12-C01, V12-C02, V12-C03, V12-C05, V12-C06, V12-C12, V12-C14.

June Park

V2 path-seer; mechanics pending world reconciliation.

Wants: Understand possible routes while remaining a companion rather than an oracle everyone must obey.

Offers: Can compare options and teach bounded observation.

Cost or danger: Seeing alternatives can turn other people's lives into a tempting optimization problem.

Internal conflict: Wants to spare a friend a mistake but cannot own the friend's choice.

Voice: Economical, alert, capable of affectionate understatement; corrects a practical error before delivering a lesson.

Chapter route: V12-C02, V12-C03, V12-C05, V12-C14.

Yuna

V2 supporting cast child oracle; new refusal episode proposed.

Wants: Have an ordinary future alongside unusual perception.

Offers: Sees evidence adults may miss.

Cost or danger: Adults can recruit her as proof, weapon, or mascot.

Internal conflict: Curiosity competes with a justified wish not to be responsible for everyone's safety.

Voice: Specific questions, selective silence, flashes of humour appropriate to a young person; never an adult sermon in a child's mouth.

Chapter route: V12-C04.

Malcolm Thorne

V2 antagonist; exploitative contract details proposed.

Wants: Continue living and keep control of the relationships and property he fears losing.

Offers: Can mobilize material resources quickly.

Cost or danger: Externalizes risk onto people who cannot meaningfully negotiate with him.

Internal conflict: Calls his attachment love while treating refusal and loss as unacceptable defects.

Voice: Polished reassurance, proprietary pronouns, calm managerial language that fractures when control fails.

Chapter route: V12-C07.

Firstborn dissenter

Composite editorial character; fixedness versus learning unresolved.

Wants: Prevent a preservation order from consuming the people supposedly protected.

Offers: Provides access, testimony, and a route away from institutional coercion.

Cost or danger: Defection exposes dependents and may preserve some self-serving loyalties.

Internal conflict: May reveal pre-existing dissent rather than newly acquire the capacity to change; do not choose the ontology silently.

Voice: Formal vocabulary gradually narrowed to concrete persons; not a human wisecracker with luminous decoration.

Chapter route: V12-C08.

Neris, first recorder

New editorial name for proposed first recorder; no ancient attestation.

Wants: Make a faithful record without confusing completeness with truth.

Offers: Keeps conflicting names and gaps recoverable.

Cost or danger: The desire for tidy categories can turn living differences into errors.

Internal conflict: Pride in accuracy meets the realization that omission can be more honest than invention.

Voice: Careful questions and understated literal observations; humour from incongruity, not compulsive bureaucratic jokes.

Chapter route: V01-C04, V01-C08.

Sadir, refuge quartermaster

New editorial supporting character; lifespan and appearances require local clock placement.

Wants: Keep Lilith's refuge materially viable without turning hardship into authority.

Offers: Knows roofs, routes, stores, and the limits of heroic promises.

Cost or danger: Scarcity tempts admission rules that exclude difficult needs.

Internal conflict: Loves the refuge but resents being taken for granted by its protectors.

Voice: Tactile, impatient with grand speeches during practical work; familiar teasing earned through years of repair.

Chapter route: V02-C13, V03-C13.

Enoch

1 Enoch traditions and Genesis; scene motives proposed.

Wants: Carry testimony accurately between unequal powers.

Offers: Can reach hearings ordinary petitioners cannot.

Cost or danger: Access tempts others to demand guarantees he cannot give.

Internal conflict: Empathy for frightened petitioners conflicts with the harm alleged against them.

Voice: Measured, attentive to sequence and what he did not understand; wonder without inflated certainty.

Chapter route: V03-C04, V03-C05.

Noah

Genesis flood narrative; ethical interiority proposed.

Wants: Preserve life through an event he cannot independently halt.

Offers: Organizes shelter and prolonged shared survival.

Cost or danger: Selection excludes lives; survival can become unwarranted moral prestige.

Internal conflict: Gratitude and grief remain entangled after the water retreats.

Voice: Practical instructions under strain; not a permanently serene spokesman for catastrophe.

Chapter route: V03-C07, V03-C09, V03-C10.

Abraham

Genesis; Abram/Abraham name chronology retained.

Wants: Secure a future and relationship with the encountered promise.

Offers: Can travel, negotiate, and challenge judgement.

Cost or danger: Protective strategies repeatedly transfer risk to less powerful household members.

Internal conflict: Believes a promise matters while fearing his own choices could lose it.

Voice: Hospitable negotiation mixed with evasive self-justification; rhetorical patience under public pressure.

Chapter route: V04-C01, V04-C02, V04-C05, V04-C08, V04-C09.

Sarah

Genesis; Sarai/Sarah change preserved.

Wants: Have a future that acknowledges her life already lived.

Offers: Brings household competence, scepticism, and a capacity to expose impossible assurances.

Cost or danger: Her insecurity can become domination of Hagar.

Internal conflict: Wants recognition and reproduces exclusion to secure it.

Voice: Compressed judgement, unsentimental observation, laughter with several emotional meanings.

Chapter route: V04-C02, V04-C04, V04-C07.

Hagar

Genesis; independent testimony preserved.

Wants: Keep herself and Ishmael alive beyond the household that used and expelled her.

Offers: Builds an independent lineage and naming testimony.

Cost or danger: Hard-won caution can make every offered relation look like another trap.

Internal conflict: Needs aid without accepting renewed possession.

Voice: Direct about water, travel, and the child; theological insight begins in lived need, not lecture.

Chapter route: V04-C03, V04-C07.

Joseph

Genesis; policy consequences retained.

Wants: Survive betrayal, use his ability, and bring his family through famine.

Offers: Turns interpretation and administration into large-scale provision.

Cost or danger: Centralized relief increases state ownership and dependence.

Internal conflict: Wants reunion without admitting how much he now resembles the systems that once controlled him.

Voice: Controlled courtesy, strategic delay, administrative precision; feeling leaks through when family interrupts the official role.

Chapter route: V04-C15, V04-C16.

Moses

Exodus through Deuteronomy; portrayal proposed.

Wants: Lead a liberation he did not seek and leave it able to continue.

Offers: Can confront centralized power and organize a new community.

Cost or danger: Exhaustion and authority can reproduce the coercion he opposes.

Internal conflict: Public mission competes with reluctance, anger, and limited hearing capacity.

Voice: Reluctant formulation becoming forceful address; private frustration distinct from ceremonial speech.

Chapter route: V05-C03, V05-C04, V05-C09, V05-C10, V05-C16.

Miriam

Exodus and Numbers; counter-record role proposed.

Wants: Make women's care, leadership, and losses visible inside the liberation.

Offers: Sustains public memory and collective courage.

Cost or danger: Rivalry over authority can narrow her own view of others.

Internal conflict: Committed to the movement while unwilling to disappear inside her brother's office.

Voice: Rhythmic public speech and brisk domestic directness; humour can hold shared memory without erasing fear.

Chapter route: V05-C02, V05-C07, V05-C14.

Ruth

Ruth; no retrospective assimilation of origin.

Wants: Keep Naomi and herself alive and create a durable future.

Offers: Practical loyalty transforms the meaning of kinship and belonging.

Cost or danger: Her labour can be romanticized into an endless duty to sacrifice herself.

Internal conflict: Needs a secure arrangement without becoming merely the instrument of a lineage.

Voice: Observant, economical, attentive to how a promise changes tomorrow's work.

Chapter route: V06-C14.

David

Samuel and Chronicles; distinct witnesses retained.

Wants: Survive, rule, and be recognized as legitimate.

Offers: Can exercise restraint, inspire loyalty, and consolidate institutions.

Cost or danger: Royal access enables domination, concealment, and failures toward his own household.

Internal conflict: The man who refuses a convenient killing later engineers one.

Voice: Quick social perception and genuine lyric capacity; charm becomes dangerous when it prevents honest refusal.

Chapter route: V06-C17, V06-C18, V06-C19, V06-C20.

Bathsheba

2 Samuel; interiority editorial, not recovered historical testimony.

Wants: Remain a person and secure a future after royal power remakes her household.

Offers: Can preserve memory and navigate succession.

Cost or danger: Survival in court may require compromises that later admirers oversimplify.

Internal conflict: Rejects being reduced either to seductress or passive proof of another man's repentance.

Voice: Sparse, deliberate, alert to who can safely hear a sentence.

Chapter route: V06-C19, V06-C21.

Solomon, king

Kings and Chronicles; never automatically the V2 Threshold trader.

Wants: Establish wise order and durable splendour.

Offers: Can hear a difficult case and organize immense work.

Cost or danger: Prestige accumulates labour extraction, taxation, and dynastic strain.

Internal conflict: Reputation for discernment competes with the experience of the people funding it.

Voice: Elegant comparison and confident public judgement; practical blind spots appear when the subject is his own administration.

Chapter route: V06-C21, V06-C22.

Jesus

Distinct Gospel witnesses; incarnation bounded by approved cosmology.

Wants: Teach and enact relation without turning power into coercive rule.

Offers: Changes obligations through encounter, healing, teaching, and care.

Cost or danger: Followers can turn his language or absence into authority he did not grant them.

Internal conflict: Public urgency coexists with embodied fatigue, fear, and the limits witnesses can know.

Voice: Concrete images, questions, parables, direct rebukes, tenderness; avoid generic cosmic exposition and imported author mannerisms.

Chapter route: V09-C03, V09-C04, V09-C06, V09-C10, V09-C12, V09-C16, V09-C18.

Mary Magdalene

Distinct Gospel witnesses; no invented certainty about gaps.

Wants: Remain faithful to what she witnessed and get it heard.

Offers: Carries consequential testimony through grief and disbelief.

Cost or danger: Others may use her witness while reducing her independent standing.

Internal conflict: Needs an account of absence without knowing in advance what she will encounter.

Voice: Immediate sensory precision, direct corrections, grief allowed to interrupt fluency.

Chapter route: V09-C19, V09-C21.

Paul

Acts and epistles; attribution and chronology differences preserved.

Wants: Build and sustain communities around a transformed conviction.

Offers: Creates connections, arguments, aid routes, and durable correspondence.

Cost or danger: Rhetoric can outlive its context and support hierarchies in recipients' lives.

Internal conflict: Urgent persuasion competes with accountability for prior harm and current limits.

Voice: Argumentative momentum, compressed pivots, appeals to relationship; recipient voices must not become echoes.

Chapter route: V10-C05, V10-C07, V10-C08, V10-C12, V10-C20.

Phoebe

Romans 16; delivery and performance details proposed.

Wants: Bring an argument and a network of people into a divided community's hearing.

Offers: Connects interpretation with named relationships and actual travel.

Cost or danger: Messenger prestige could narrow who gets to answer.

Internal conflict: Wants the letter understood without claiming every response as her own.

Voice: Composed and situationally exact; capable of restoring context through one well-placed question.

Chapter route: V10-C13.

Onesimus

Philemon and related textual references; outcome not presumed.

Wants: Obtain a future beyond possession by another person.

Offers: Exposes the difference between an appeal and a changed condition.

Cost or danger: Others may use his situation to demonstrate their benevolence.

Internal conflict: Must assess risk under a correspondence he did not author.

Voice: Careful about promises, specific about conditions, neither grateful mascot nor flawless symbolic rebel.

Chapter route: V10-C14.

Ione, Corinthian worker

New editorial name for poor Corinthian member.

Wants: Eat and belong after a day's work.

Offers: Makes ritual exclusion visible through ordinary bodily need.

Cost or danger: Exhaustion can make sustained participation costly.

Internal conflict: Wants community while resenting the burden of teaching it what hunger means.

Voice: Plain, wry among friends, precise when polite evasions appear.

Chapter route: V10-C10, V10-C11.

Samira, manuscript custodian

New editorial late transmission character; no invented historical attribution.

Wants: Keep a contested archive usable without surrendering it to its patron.

Offers: Preserves terminology, copies, and independent custody.

Cost or danger: Care for records can conflict with the urgent safety of living custodians.

Internal conflict: Loves the collection but must accept that preservation cannot always mean keeping it in one place.

Voice: Patient explanation, dry affection for colleagues, firm refusal of false equivalence.

Chapter route: V11-C04, V11-C13.

Nadia, repair coordinator

New editorial modern supporting character.

Wants: Make the changed world's routes and care practices actually work.

Offers: Turns revelations into completed repairs and accountable logistics.

Cost or danger: Can become impatient with grief that does not fit a schedule.

Internal conflict: Needs measurable progress without declaring unmeasurable loss finished.

Voice: Practical, companionable, capable of interrupting grandeur with a real need rather than a canned gag.

Chapter route: V12-C05, V12-C11, V12-C13, V12-C14.

Factions

Garden stewardship office

Editorial institution around Genesis and author Garden.

Wants: Preserve a successful enclosure and the authority to define its order.

Offers: Maintains provision, shelter, and a shared operating routine.

Cost or danger: Makes belonging conditional on compliance with imposed roles.

Internal conflict: Some stewards want interpretable rules; others treat questioning as system failure.

Voice: Assuring, procedural, offended by questions it cannot classify.

Chapter route: V01-C09, V02-C04, V02-C08, V02-C09.

Lilith's refuge network

New editorial institution across the series.

Wants: Keep displaced people alive without buying obedience.

Offers: Provides routes, roofs, reciprocal care, and independent testimony.

Cost or danger: Scarcity strains generosity; protectors can accumulate informal power.

Internal conflict: Quartermasters demand workable limits while others resist any admission rule.

Voice: Practical familiarity, disagreement with affection, jokes tied to shared labour.

Chapter route: V02-C13, V03-C13, V04-C17, V05-C17, V06-C23, V07-C19, V08-C19, V09-C21, V10-C21, V11-C15, V12-C13.

Watcher patronage circles

1 Enoch adaptation; not all angelic or divine beings.

Wants: Extend influence through instruction and privileged relation.

Offers: Offer arts and knowledge capable of improving life.

Cost or danger: Tie access to coercive personal obligations and unequal dependence.

Internal conflict: Teachers who want reciprocity clash with patrons who treat dependence as proof of gratitude.

Voice: Paternal assurance, technical confidence, injured generosity when challenged.

Chapter route: V03-C01, V03-C02, V03-C04, V03-C06.

Egyptian grain and labour court

Editorial institutional focus within Genesis and Exodus; not all Egyptians.

Wants: Keep state continuity, food supply, and elite control.

Offers: Can coordinate storage and material distribution at scale.

Cost or danger: Turns emergency dependence into enduring extraction and hereditary coercion.

Internal conflict: Administrators pursuing survival conflict with elites prioritizing command.

Voice: Numbers used as arguments; polite euphemisms for burdens assigned downward.

Chapter route: V04-C16, V05-C01, V05-C04, V05-C05, V05-C06.

Covenant households

Genesis family coalitions; not a homogeneous ethnic essence.

Wants: Secure land, descendants, protection, and continuity of promise.

Offers: Provide loyalty, mobility, practical care, and inherited testimony.

Cost or danger: Privilege lineage, status, and control of marriage or reproduction.

Internal conflict: Hagar, Sarah, children, servants, and patriarchs hold incompatible stakes.

Voice: Kinship language, negotiated promises, old jokes with long memories.

Chapter route: V04-C01, V04-C03, V04-C04, V04-C07, V04-C14, V04-C16.

Wilderness assembly and service offices

Exodus-Leviticus-Numbers-Deuteronomy adaptation.

Wants: Make collective freedom livable after escape.

Offers: Creates distribution, courts, sanctuary work, and shared expectations.

Cost or danger: Rules can harden into unequal status and immune leadership.

Internal conflict: Caregivers, daughters, artisans, priests, and commanders dispute what freedom requires.

Voice: Public exhortation contrasted with informal queues, workshops, and households.

Chapter route: V05-C08, V05-C09, V05-C10, V05-C11, V05-C13, V05-C14, V05-C15.

Royal and sanctuary establishments

Distinct kingdoms must remain historically and narratively separable.

Wants: Consolidate legitimacy, defence, taxation, and sacred prestige.

Offers: Can support infrastructure, justice, and collective memory.

Cost or danger: Extract labour, suppress complaint, and conflate divine favour with success.

Internal conflict: Prophets, court workers, relatives, debtors, and soldiers contest royal conduct.

Voice: Polished confidence at the centre; sharply different registers among those doing its work.

Chapter route: V06-C16, V06-C18, V06-C19, V06-C22, V07-C01, V07-C03, V07-C11.

Dispersed testimony circles

Editorial coalition within exile and diaspora; not one historical organization.

Wants: Preserve continuity without intact territory or sanctuary.

Offers: Keeps texts, lament, practical wisdom, songs, and household stories.

Cost or danger: Curators may privilege respectable memory over difficult witnesses.

Internal conflict: Restoration advocates conflict with those who cannot or do not wish to return.

Voice: Many registers deliberately retained; one authorized voice would betray the institution's function.

Chapter route: V07-C14, V07-C16, V07-C18, V07-C19, V08-C01, V08-C04.

Early assembly networks

Acts and epistles; local communities remain distinct.

Wants: Sustain witness, belonging, and aid across distance.

Offers: Creates hospitality, collections, teaching, and relations beyond some inherited boundaries.

Cost or danger: Charisma, wealth, household status, and office can become domination.

Internal conflict: Recipients answer messengers; poor members challenge meals; hosts contest controlling leaders.

Voice: Argument, personal greeting, correction, friendship, and exasperation; no universal church voice.

Chapter route: V10-C01, V10-C03, V10-C07, V10-C10, V10-C14, V10-C18.

Exclusive archive patronages

Composite editorial antagonistic institution, not a whole religion.

Wants: Maintain legitimacy through control of surviving testimony.

Offers: Funds preservation, buildings, copies, and specialist labour.

Cost or danger: Makes access and survival conditional on doctrinal assimilation.

Internal conflict: Custodians wanting accurate records resist benefactors seeking unanimous ancestry.

Voice: Generous public language with increasingly specific private exceptions.

Chapter route: V08-C19, V11-C02, V11-C03, V11-C13, V11-C15.

Firstborn preservation hierarchy

Author source institution; membership and capacity conflict unresolved.

Wants: Keep its assigned order continuous and its own offices necessary.

Offers: Maintains some routes and structures on which others depend.

Cost or danger: Treats controlled history and institutional survival as justification for coercive preservation.

Internal conflict: Existing dissent versus capacity to change must be settled by source reconciliation; not assumed.

Voice: Formal mandate language; panic appears as procedural escalation.

Chapter route: V01-C09, V12-C08.

Recovery coalition

V2 seeds plus original cross-tradition composition.

Wants: Investigate shared crises without surrendering distinct histories and agency.

Offers: Combines otherwise unavailable maps, practices, evidence, and practical care.

Cost or danger: Coordination can centralize authority or turn traditions into useful accessories.

Internal conflict: Members differ on risk, translation, hospitality, timing, and the meaning of repair.

Voice: Natural camaraderie among people who disagree; vocabulary remains character-specific.

Chapter route: V11-C05, V11-C06, V11-C07, V11-C08, V11-C09, V11-C10, V11-C11, V11-C12, V12-C05, V12-C11.

Thorne's continuity enterprise

Editorial development of V2 antagonist.

Wants: Secure private immortality and control of loss.

Offers: Mobilizes wealth, facilities, and technical competence.

Cost or danger: Transfers bodily and cognitive risk onto dependent people.

Internal conflict: Some workers want legitimate care; leaders frame exit as betrayal.

Voice: Reassuring service language concealing whose continuity receives priority.

Chapter route: V12-C03, V12-C07.

The Architect's builders

V2-derived proposal; Eve linkage not automatic historical truth.

Wants: Break a coercive order and make another future possible.

Offers: Understands structural failures the official keepers deny.

Cost or danger: A remedy can make existing people expendable to an imagined liberation.

Internal conflict: Members divide over total erasure, bounded change, and accountability after intervention.

Voice: Exact diagnoses, impatience with platitude, internal arguments about means.

Chapter route: V12-C09, V12-C11, V12-C13.
